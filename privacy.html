Privacy Policy

This policy describes the limited private developer Sandbox test of DJ Ghostly Dashboard (the “Dashboard”). The Dashboard is locally operated by DJ Ghostly and is not currently available to public users.

Information processed by the Dashboard

When the authorized operator connects a TikTok Sandbox account, the Dashboard may process the account's basic profile identity, authorization tokens, current creator publishing options, approved video files, captions, hashtags, disclosure selections, upload progress, and resulting post identifiers or status.

The Dashboard does not intentionally collect information from third-party users. It does not provide public registration, advertising, behavioral tracking, or a public user database.

How information is used

Information is used only to confirm the intended account, show the publishing choices TikTok currently permits, upload a video selected and approved by the operator, recover an interrupted local upload, and display the resulting status inside the Dashboard.

Local storage and transmission

Application credentials, authorization tokens, video files, settings, and upload-recovery records are stored on the operator's computer in local profile data and backups. They are not stored in this website or its public source repository. When the operator explicitly approves a post, the necessary account information, publishing selections, and video data are transmitted directly to TikTok's APIs.

Sharing and sale

Personal information is not sold, rented, or used for advertising. Information is shared with TikTok only as necessary to authenticate the authorized account and perform an operator-approved action. This informational website is hosted by GitHub Pages; DJ Ghostly has added no cookies, analytics, forms, or tracking scripts to it. GitHub may process ordinary hosting and access information under its own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Local authorization and workflow data remain until the operator disconnects the account, deletes the relevant local profile or records, or removes associated backups. Completed publishing records may remain locally so the operator can identify previously posted content. TikTok retains information according to its own policies.

Security

The Dashboard keeps credentials and tokens outside its public website and source-control history. Access is limited to the operator's local computer and selected profile. No security method can guarantee absolute protection, but the project is designed to minimize stored data and requested permissions.

Children

The Dashboard is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the Sandbox scope or Dashboard behavior changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests concerning locally controlled Dashboard data can be sent to dj.ghostly.dj@gmail.com.
